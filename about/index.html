comma.ai

                    
I'll be a datacenter intern at comma.ai this summer. They make openpilot, open-source driver assistance for 350+ car models, and they train everything in their own datacenter.

                

                

                    
SculptorAI

                    
SculptorAI is a registered nonprofit AI research lab. I co-founded it and I'm the CEO, and we release our work as open source.

                    
Our research covers diffusion language models, autonomous driving, and a few science problems, like predicting the atmospheres of brown dwarfs and forecasting how long an apple will keep. I trained and released the Ursa model family. More at sculptorai.org.

                    
On the driving side there's TurnPilot, our fork of openpilot. We rebuilt Navigate on openpilot inside BeamNG.drive and wrote up what should carry over to a real car.

                    
We also released Indra, a family of tiny, general weather models at 3 million and 1.5 million parameters, and the small one is a 6 MB file. We've only tested them at airports so far, and at 12 airports they'd never seen, their temperature error was 11.7% and 10.3% lower than the National Blend. Indra-3M also runs the forecast at weather.explodingcb.com.
