Tabletop Tyrant

A tiny village settles on your real desk. Your webcam watches your hands, and you decide what happens to it.

  1. Point your camera at the table in front of you.
  2. Slide a flat hand across the table so the game can find it.
  3. Drag the line onto the edge of your desk. The village fills the rest.

Works best in Chrome on a laptop. The video stays on your device; nothing is uploaded. On a MacBook, turn off Center Stage in the Video menu so the camera shows the whole table.
