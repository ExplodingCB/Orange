Stillfall Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Stillfall is a puzzle game. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Your progress

Your settings, best scores and earned backgrounds are stored only on your device.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards so you can compare them with other players. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; Stillfall does not receive or store any of your Game Center information.

In-app purchases

Skins and tips are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Stillfall only learns which items you own so it can unlock them.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

Stillfall has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Stillfall does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
