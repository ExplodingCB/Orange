Stillfall

A calm falling-block puzzle for iPhone and iPad.

Stack the falling pieces, clear lines, and play at your own pace in three modes: Classic, Time Trial and Zen.

Support

Something broken, or have a question? Email chase@explodingcb.com and I'll get back to you.

Skins and tips

Skins and tips are one-time in-app purchases. If you reinstall the game or move to a new device, open the Shop and tap Restore purchases to get them back. Backgrounds that come with a tip return the same way.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards use Game Center. If they don't appear, check that you're signed in to Game Center in the Settings app.
