FobCam

A tiny keychain digicam for your iPhone.

FobCam runs every photo through a full simulation of a little 1.6 megapixel keychain camera: soft grain, slow-shutter blur, the orange date stamp and crunchy little videos.

Common questions

Where do my photos go? Every finished photo and video is saved to your Photos library, and kept in FobCam's Playback screen. Deleting one in Playback leaves the copy in Photos.

The camera says it's unavailable. Open Settings, find FobCam, and turn on Camera. Turn on Microphone too if you want sound in videos.

Photos aren't showing up in my library. Open Settings, find FobCam, and allow it to add to Photos.

Can I use a photo I already took? Yes. Open Playback and tap Import to run any photo from your library through the camera.
