FobCam Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

FobCam is a camera app. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Photos and videos

Everything you shoot or import is processed on your iPhone. Finished photos and videos are saved to your Photos library and kept inside the app so you can view them in Playback. They never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

Camera, microphone and Photos access

FobCam uses the camera to take photos and videos, the microphone only to record sound with videos, and Photos access only to save your shots and to import a photo you pick.

Motion sensors

FobCam reads your phone's motion sensors to orient photos and to recreate hand-shake blur. This data is used on the device during a shot and is not stored or sent anywhere.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

FobCam has no network code, no third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

FobCam does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
